Sample 1: Notice of Intent to Sue for Breach of Contract – Failure to Deliver Services
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State, ZIP Code]
[Email Address]
[Phone Number]

[Date]

[Recipient’s Name]
[Recipient’s Company]
[Recipient’s Address]
[City, State, ZIP Code]

Subject: Notice of Intent to Sue for Breach of Contract

Dear [Recipient’s Name],

I am writing to formally notify you of my intent to initiate legal action due to your failure to deliver the contracted services as outlined in our agreement dated [Contract Date]. Despite repeated requests and attempts to resolve this matter amicably, you have not fulfilled your obligations, specifically [describe breach, e.g., failure to provide agreed consulting services].

This breach has caused me significant financial loss and disruption to my business operations. Unless the matter is resolved within 15 days from the date of this letter, I will have no option but to pursue all available legal remedies to recover damages and enforce the contract.

Please consider this letter a final opportunity to cure the breach and avoid litigation.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

Sample 2: Letter of Intent to Sue for Breach of Contract – Nonpayment
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State, ZIP Code]
[Email Address]
[Phone Number]

[Date]

[Recipient’s Name]
[Recipient’s Company]
[Recipient’s Address]
[City, State, ZIP Code]

Subject: Notice of Intent to Sue for Breach of Contract Due to Nonpayment

Dear [Recipient’s Name],

This letter serves as formal notice of my intent to file a lawsuit for breach of contract stemming from your failure to remit payment as agreed upon in our contract dated [Contract Date]. The amount of [$Amount] remains unpaid despite multiple reminders.

I request that you make full payment within 10 business days from receipt of this letter. If payment is not received within this time frame, I will proceed with legal action to recover the outstanding balance, including any additional costs incurred.

I urge you to treat this matter with urgency to avoid further consequences.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

Sample 3: Notice of Intent to Sue for Breach of Contract – Defective Goods
[Your Name]
[Your Address]
[City, State, ZIP Code]
[Email Address]
[Phone Number]

[Date]

[Recipient’s Name]
[Recipient’s Company]
[Recipient’s Address]
[City, State, ZIP Code]

Subject: Intent to Initiate Legal Proceedings for Breach of Contract

Dear [Recipient’s Name],




I am writing to inform you of my intent to pursue legal action due to your delivery of defective goods in violation of our sales contract dated [Contract Date]. The products delivered on [Delivery Date] failed to meet the specifications agreed upon, specifically [describe defects].

Despite my attempts to obtain a remedy, you have not resolved the issue or offered a satisfactory replacement or refund. I demand that you take corrective action within 14 days or face formal litigation.

This letter constitutes my final attempt to resolve this dispute without court intervention.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
