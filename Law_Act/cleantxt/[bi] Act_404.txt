5Racing Club (Public Sweepstakes) LAWS OF MALAYSIA Act 404 RACING CLUB (PUBLIC SWEEPSTAKES) ACT 1965 An Act to empower racing clubs to promote public sweepstakes. [Peninsular Malaysia—1 August 1965, L.N 298/1965; Sabah, Sarawak and Federal Territory of Labuan—15 April 1992, P.U. (B) 163/1992 ] BE IT ENACTED by the Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows: Short title and application 1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Racing Club (Public Sweepstakes) Act 1965. (2) This Act shall apply throughout Malaysia. Racing Clubs may promote public sweepstakes 2. (1) A racing club established in Malaysia may, after the commencement of this Act, promote a sweeptake in which any person (including a person not being a member of the club) may take part; and the *Betting Ordinance 1953 [ Ord. 47 of 1953 ], shall not apply to any sweepstake so promoted. (2) In this section— “racing club” means a racing club which is a member of the Malayan Racing Association or a successor thereof; and “sweepstakes” means any sweepstake promoted on the result of a horse race. 3. (Omitted ). *NOTE—The Betting Ordinance 1953 [ Ord. 47 of 1953 ] has been revised as the Betting Act 1953 [Act 495 ].
