Legislative of Sarawak (Application of Monies Borrowed from the Federation) 5 LAWS OF MALAYSIA Act 779 LEGISLATURE OF SARAWAK (APPLICATION OF MONIES BORROWED FROM THE FEDERATION) ACT 1968 An Act to authorise the Legislature of Sarawak to make laws with respect to the application of monies borrowed under the Loan (School Managements) Ordinance 1964 of the State of Sarawak for certain purposes. [25 February 1964 ] Short title 1. This Act may be cited as the Legislature of Sarawak (Application of Monies Borrowed from the Federation) Act 1968. Authorisation 2. In accordance with Article 76a of the Federal Constitution the Legislature of Sarawak is authorised to make laws in respect to the application of the monies borrowed under the Loan (School Managements) Ordinance 1964 of Sarawak [ Sarawak 2 of 1964 ] for the purposes and in accordance with the Loans to Aided Schools Regulations 1956 [ GN. S. 135/1956 ] of that State.
