Misleading indication as to price 12. (1) A person commits an offence— (a) if he gives to a consumer an indication which is misleading as to the price at which any goods or services are available; or (b) if an indication given by him to a consumer as to the price at which any goods or services are available becomes misleading and he fails to take reasonable steps to prevent the consumer from relying on the indication.
