5 LAWS OF MALAYSI A Act 217 DECLARATION OF AN AREA IN THE BINTULU DISTRICT TO BE A FEDERAL PORT ACT 1979 An Act to provid e for the declaratio n of an area in the Bintul u Distric t in the Fourt h Divisio n of the State of Sarawa k to be a federal port, for the transfer to and vesting in the Federal Government of responsibilit y for the development , contro l and administratio n of such port and for all other matter s and thing s necessar y therefor , incidenta l theret o or connecte d therewith . [15 Augus t 1981] BE IT ENACTE D by the Seri Paduk a Bagind a Yang di-Pertua n Agong with the advice and consen t of the Dewan Negar a and Dewan Rakyat in Parliamen t assembled , and by the authorit y of the same, as follows : Short title 1. This Act may be cited as the Declaratio n of an Area in the Bintul u Distric t to be a Federa l Port Act 1979. Interpretatio n 2. In this Act, “Minister ” means the Federa l Ministe r charge d with responsibilit y for ports and harbours . Declaratio n of area to be federa l port by Ministe r 3. The Ministe r may with effect from such date as he may specify , by order in the Gazette , declar e an area in the Bintul u Distric t in the Fourth Divisio n of the State of Sarawa k to be a federa l port to be known as Bintul u Port.
